## 🚀 Introduction
Waves Mercury for Windows is a broad plug-in suite designed for engineers who want a complete processing arsenal available inside their DAW. Instead of describing a single tool, it is best understood by its categories: equalizers, compressors and other dynamics, reverbs, delays, modulation, saturation, and channel processing. Among these you'll find the Waves Audio Mercury dynamics plug-ins that many mix engineers rely on for level control and punch. Because everything loads as native plug-ins in your host application, you can build entire mixes, refine buses, and finalize masters without stepping outside your session.

## ✨ Highlights

### Precision and Control
- **Dynamics processing** — compressors, limiters, and gates to manage level, transients, and sustain.
- **Detailed equalization** — corrective and musical EQ options for cleaning up and sculpting tone.
- **Consistent recall** — every processor saves and restores with your DAW project.

### Space and Character
- **Reverbs and delays** — time-based effects for depth, width, and ambience across a mix.
- **Color and movement** — saturation and modulation options for adding warmth and interest.

## 💻 System Specs
**Minimum**
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Host application: a DAW supporting VST3 or AAX plug-ins
- Processor: a modern 64-bit multi-core CPU
- Memory: your DAW's minimum supported RAM
- Storage: several gigabytes of free space for installation

**Recommended**
- OS: Windows 11 64-bit
- Host application: a current release of your preferred VST3/AAX-compatible DAW
- Processor: a recent high-performance multi-core CPU
- Memory: 16 GB or more for large sessions
- Storage: an SSD with ample free space for plug-in content

## 🎯 Getting Up and Running
1. Launch your DAW and open a project.
2. Choose a track or bus and open an insert slot.
3. Select a Waves Mercury plug-in from your DAW's effects list.
4. Fine-tune the controls, then automate or store presets within the session.

## ❓ Common Questions
**Is Waves Mercury free?**
No. Waves Mercury is a paid plug-in suite from Waves Audio. Acquire it through official channels to ensure a valid, licensed copy.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
It supports modern 64-bit Windows releases such as Windows 10 and Windows 11, running inside a DAW that supports VST3 or AAX plug-ins.

**Is my data secure?**
Audio is processed locally within your DAW on your own computer. Keep your license and downloads official and follow standard security practices.
